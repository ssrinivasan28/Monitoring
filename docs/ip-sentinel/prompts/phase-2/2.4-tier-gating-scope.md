# 2.4 — Pro-tier gating + customer data-only scope

*Prepend [`00-shared-preamble.md`](../00-shared-preamble.md).*

**Goal:** enforce the Assistant as the **Pro unlock**, and constrain the **customer** Assistant to
their own data.

**Deliverables**
- Entitlement gate (from 0.3) on all Assistant endpoints/surfaces (SPA + Teams).
- **Scope policy:** customer Assistant answers **from their tenant data only** (no general-knowledge
  speculation); staff Assistant may use broader best-practice knowledge.
- Disclaimers on AI answers; "insufficient data" instead of guessing.

**Acceptance**
- Basic tenant → Assistant blocked everywhere (server-enforced, 403 + upsell).
- Customer Assistant refuses/redirects out-of-scope questions and answers only from their data (test).
